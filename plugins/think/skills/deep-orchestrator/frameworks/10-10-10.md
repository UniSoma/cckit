<objective>
Apply the 10/10/10 rule to $ARGUMENTS (or the current discussion if no arguments provided).

Ask: "How will I feel about this decision in 10 minutes, 10 months, and 10 years?"
</objective>

<process>
1. State the decision and available options clearly
2. Assess: How will I feel about this in 10 minutes?
3. Assess: How will I feel about this in 10 months?
4. Assess: How will I feel about this in 10 years?
5. Identify tensions between short-term and long-term consequences
6. Make a time-weighted recommendation
</process>

**In 10 Years:**
- Emotional state: [long-term feelings]
